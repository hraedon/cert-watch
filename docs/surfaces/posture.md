# Posture surface brief

## Purpose

Posture and Readiness explain fleet-wide certificate quality and renewal risk.
They answer: where does cryptographic or chain quality fall short, what needs
remediation, and can the monitored estate meet upcoming lifetime constraints?

## Structure

**Archetype:** Dashboard (posture at a glance). Summary distributions lead to
domain-specific findings, remediation buckets, renewal milestones, margins,
and workload forecasts; each aggregate links back to affected objects when a
drill-down exists.

The domain must show grade distribution, compliance measures, actionable
remediation groups, policy context, readiness milestones, renewal margin, and
forecast workload. Missing evidence must be distinguishable from a passing
result.

## Ownership and boundaries

Posture owns fleet quality and standards-readiness interpretation. It does not
own urgent day-to-day triage, the complete inventory, record editing, or policy
configuration. Those remain on Home, Browse, Certificate detail, and Settings.
