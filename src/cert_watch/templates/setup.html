Comma-separated CIDR ranges cert-watch may scan (e.g. 10.0.0.0/8, 192.168.0.0/16); leave blank to allow all private (RFC 1918) addresses. You can change this later under Settings or via the CERT_WATCH_ALLOWED_SUBNETS environment variable.
You can also configure authentication via environment variables (AUTH_PROVIDER, CERT_WATCH_LOCAL_ADMIN_USER, CERT_WATCH_LOCAL_ADMIN_PASSWORD_HASH). See the runbook for details.
