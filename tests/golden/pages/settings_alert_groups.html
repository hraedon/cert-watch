Alert groups

per-team alert routing, matched by tag
Matching groups add their email recipients in addition to global SMTP recipients and the certificate's owner/team contacts. A certificate matching several groups produces one alert with their combined recipients. Groups can also set an alert threshold and a shared weekly digest window. Group webhooks are not used for delivery; configure the global webhook in Channels.
